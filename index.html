About the research

This report applies Ehud Hemli's Data Fluidity methodology (DUR Principle) to Israeli governance. The core measurement: F = D × U / R — value fluidity as a function of decision quality (D) × realized public utility (U) ÷ systemic friction (R).

Every unit of analysis — each prime minister, public system, sector, company, union, NGO — is scored on 17 quantitative indicators drawn strictly from primary sources: Israeli government records, regulatory filings, international indices, and peer-reviewed research.

The research is independent and reproducible. All 40 source documents, 17 calculation algorithms, and raw data are published under the MIT license. Every number traces back to its source.
