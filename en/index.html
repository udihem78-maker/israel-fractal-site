02 · Findings

Five critical diagnoses.

1

A fractal gap of one hundred and seventy.

In seventy-eight years, from the provisional government to Netanyahu's sixth term, Israel's F score fell from 100 to 0.6. The curve is exponential — this is a trend, not an accident.

2

A third of institutions already in collapse.

Of fifteen public systems analyzed, six operate below their collapse threshold: the police (F=5), Southern Command (8), the state prosecution (8), the Rabbinate (8), education (10), and welfare (10).

3

Sixty-five percent of sectors — in collapse.

Palestinians in Gaza live at F=0.1. Bedouin at F=1. Native secular Israelis at F=1.7. Internal variance: two hundred and sixty-seven times. Israel-Palestine is the most fragmented society in the Western world.

4

Cumulative damage: two trillion shekels.

Nineteen years of Benjamin Netanyahu's rule. Cumulative net fiscal impact: minus 666 billion. Indirect damage: minus 210 billion. Debt accumulated: 735 billion. Total: ninety-three percent of one year's GDP.

5

The citizen is fourteen times stronger than the government.

The average Israeli citizen operates at F=8.5. The current government, at F=0.6. The gap is substantial. The fate of Israel is not in the hands of those who govern. It is in the hands of those who elect them.
